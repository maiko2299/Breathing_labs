### Why Breathwork?

Breathing is the only autonomic nervous system function that we also have conscious control over. By manually overriding your breathing patterns, you can:
- Lower your heart rate instantly.
- Enter flow states for deep work.
- Induce states of mild euphoria safely.

We built the [Breathing App](/breathe) specifically to help you guide these sessions perfectly, with no distractions.
