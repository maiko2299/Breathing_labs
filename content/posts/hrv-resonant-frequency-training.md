# Personalized Resonant-Frequency Breathing (HRV Training Module)

**Tier:** Advanced  
**Primary Use Cases:** Deep mental reset, chronic anxiety management, autonomic training  

## Mechanism (Summary)

HRV biofeedback protocols identify a person’s unique resonance frequency—typically between 4.5–6.5 breaths per minute—at which HRV amplitude and baroreflex gain are maximized, leading to improved emotional regulation and stress resilience. HRV biofeedback interventions using resonance-frequency breathing reduce anxiety, depression, and improve outcomes in cardiac and respiratory disorders.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

In a calibration session, guide users through several breathing rates (e.g., 4.5, 5.0, 5.5, 6.0 breaths/min), each for a few minutes, while collecting HRV data from a compatible wearable.

1. Identify the rate with the highest HRV amplitude / optimal LF/HF pattern.
2. Save this as the user’s **"Resonant Rate."**
3. For regular sessions, guide inhalation and exhalation equally around this rate (e.g., 5s in / 5s out).

*(Note: In the Breathing Labs App, you can currently use our standard 5.5s In / 5.5s Out Resonant-Frequency protocol built into the Advanced tier, which approximates this rate for most users!)*

## Session Guidance

- **Length:** 10–20 minutes.
- **Frequency:** 3–5×/week for chronic anxiety or performance use.

## Benefits & Rationale

- Resonance-frequency breathing increased HRV and improved mood more than non-resonant control breathing in randomized experiments.
- Higher HRV achieved at the end of practice predicted lower blood pressure reactivity to subsequent stressors.

## Contraindications / Notes

- As with other HRV protocols, avoid in people with significant cardiac arrhythmias unless supervised medically.
- Over-breathing risk is mitigated by coaching shallow breaths with focus on rhythm, not volume.

## Variations & Progressions

- Add cognitive tasks (e.g., reappraisal prompts) while maintaining resonance breathing for advanced users.
