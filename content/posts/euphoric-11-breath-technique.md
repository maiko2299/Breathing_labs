## How to Practice the 11-Breath Euphoric Protocol

### Phase 1: The 11 Activating Breaths
1. Sit or lie comfortably with a tall, relaxed spine.
2. **Inhale** slowly and fully through your nose for **4 seconds**.
3. **Exhale** quickly and forcefully through your mouth with a sharp **“HA!”** sound (like a mini release or laugh).
4. Repeat this rhythm **11 times**.

These breaths wake up your system, clear stale air, and create momentum.

### Phase 2: The Euphoric Retention (The 11th Breath Transition)
After your 11th “ha” exhale:
- Take a **full, deep inhale through your mouth**, filling your lungs completely.
- Hold the breath and **squeeze** — engage your abs, arms, legs, and feet (you can even point or flex your toes). Create full-body tension.
- Hold this squeezed state for **4–8 seconds**.
- Slowly release the breath in a long, controlled exhale until your lungs are completely empty.
- Then **hold the empty lungs** for as long as feels comfortable.

This is where many people feel the euphoric shift — the combination of oxygen, muscle engagement, and the empty hold creates a beautiful internal state.

### Using the “I Need to Take a Breath” Button in the App

The app will guide you to:

This button gives you full control over the retention phase while keeping the overall structure intact.

### Building Your Practice: 4 Cycles → 8 Cycles
- **Beginners**: Start with **4 complete cycles** (each cycle = 11 breaths + retention).
- **Intermediate/Advanced**: Work your way up to **8 cycles**.

The app tracks your progress, offers gentle timers, and helps you build tolerance safely over time. Most people notice significant shifts within the first week of consistent practice.
